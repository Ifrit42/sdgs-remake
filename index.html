About SDGs

Five Pillars of Sustainable Development

The 2030 Agenda is built on balance between social, economic, and environmental dimensions — expressed through five interconnected pillars.

People

Human well-being

End poverty and hunger, ensure healthy lives and quality education for all.

Planet

Environmental sustainability

Protect land and ocean ecosystems, climate action, and responsible consumption.

Prosperity

Inclusive growth

Inclusive economic growth, decent work, industry, innovation, and resilient infrastructure.

Peace

Just and inclusive societies

Peaceful, inclusive, and just societies with accountable institutions at all levels.

Partnership

Global cooperation

Revitalize global partnerships for inclusive and equitable sustainable development.
